01-hotel-lobby — 灰暗版旅店大厅
============================================================

Use case: lighting-weather / precise palette edit.
The supplied local PNG is the EDIT TARGET: an approved concept image from the fictional game "check in，please".
The user likes the image but finds it far too bright and asks for a distinctly greyer, darker, more oppressive overall atmosphere.

LOCK composition, camera angle, perspective, crop, image aspect ratio, building geometry, prop positions, character identity, silhouette shapes and count, paper layouts, ALL written Chinese/English text, numbers, dates, labels and functional UI. Characters remain the same abstract coal-black symbols with tiny pale eye marks; do not add any facial details or anatomy. No new props or story elements. No change to the fictional three-bar state seal, posters or slogans. This is a controlled atmospheric revision of the existing image.

ART DIRECTION: northern bureaucratic dystopia, heavy overcast, cold institutional grey-green and charcoal, drained and oppressive. Clearly visibly darker than the supplied image at a glance. Reduce saturation strongly, especially orange/brown warmth and red propaganda surfaces. Red becomes dirty desaturated iron-oxide, wood becomes ash-brown, paper becomes greyed and aged. Replace bright diffuse ambient fill with very low cold diffuse light, deeper directional shadow, and only a few small weak practical light pools. Light is scarce and strictly localized. Eliminate polished shiny-floor highlights and cheerful cream-white broad areas. Keep the same inhabited hotel rather than a ruin.

READABILITY: maintain enough local mid-value separation around black character silhouettes, furniture edges, walking paths and interactive objects; use subdued physically motivated floor/window bounce, never bright neon outlines. UI text and document details remain readable with their original hierarchy. Do not make the entire image pitch-black or apply a uniform black veil. No exaggerated vignette, no hazy fog, no horror effects, no new blood/gore, no bright blue neon, no new photorealism, no added film borders or watermark. Preserve the established flat graphic / textured 2.5D art style.

SCREEN-SPECIFIC CHANGES:
Environment lighting edit. Make lobby ambient illumination roughly 1.5 stops lower in perceived brightness, as a genuinely dim winter morning despite the unchanged clock. Window frost and outdoor snow are dull cold-grey rather than glowing white. The long staircase, upper wall corners and far right corridor recede into deep charcoal shadow. Large wall and floor planes become muted grey-green and graphite. A single weak reception desk lamp and weak vestibule light create small dirty-amber pools, with tight light falloff; ceiling fixtures give only scant dull light. Telescreen has a faint pale-grey image, tiny red indicator unchanged. The propaganda poster and rug become very desaturated dark red, text still legible. Reduce tile sheen. The player clerk and guests must still be recognizable at gameplay scale, readable against mid-dark floor. Keep foreground travel lanes visible and the entire fixed oblique camera composition identical.
