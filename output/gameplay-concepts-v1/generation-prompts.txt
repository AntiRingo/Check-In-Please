01-hotel-lobby — 旅店大厅与布置
============================================================

Use case: stylized-concept. This is an EDIT/RECOMPOSITION of the supplied approved art-direction sheet into ONE requested concept image for the original game "check in，please". Preserve its exact established art language and character abstraction; completely replace the sheet layout with the requested composition. The supplied sheet is the style and identity reference, not a layout to repeat. Output landscape 16:9, high-resolution, polished game concept presentation. No collage of unrelated screens, no mock computer monitor, no huge title taking up screen area.

WORLD / CONTINUITY: Fictional cold northern country whose state administers every aspect of life. A state-run hotel numbered "HOTEL 017", drawing on Russian and Nordic cold-climate civic architecture: thick walls, double windows with frost, centralized cast-iron radiators and visible pipes, faded institutional grey-green lower walls, worn grey linoleum, pale standardized wood furniture. Winter snow outdoors only. Grounded mid-20th-century analog technology. A consistent fictional state seal is a circle containing THREE straight vertical bars. State propaganda uses a perfectly symmetrical abstract black oval-headed leader icon with two tiny pale eye marks and broad shoulders, NO realistic face. Short original slogans: "秩序即安宁" and "如实登记". Posters, wired loudspeakers, wired metal-framed 4:3 telescreens, reporting boxes, ration papers. Muted oxide red for state authority, restrained and not everywhere. Quiet oppressive inhabited order, not a ruin.

CHARACTER STYLE IS LOCKED: people are symbolic solid-coal-black figures with only 4-6 large geometric silhouette masses. Black rounded or angular heads, no exposed skin, no nose, mouth, ears, brows, detailed hair, fingers, facial planes, clothes folds or realistic anatomy. ONLY two tiny muted ivory horizontal eye marks when facing the camera; no eyes on the back of heads. No smile. Modestly elongated human rather than cute mascot proportions. Clothing identified by one grey collar/scarf/hat shape. SAME three designs across all images: hotel clerk is short, broad, round-shouldered, oval head, small ivory triangular collar; Guest A is medium height, angular hood/head contour, one grey scarf and simple grey shoulder bag; Guest B is tall, narrow, plain flat winter cap, long rectangular coat, small black rectangular briefcase. Small characters and close-ups share the SAME minimal details. The user has approved this style; do not add facial anatomy even in portraits.

RENDER LANGUAGE: flat graphic shapes with subtle dry paper/plaster texture, limited hard-edged lighting, art-directed 2.5D depth but still distinctly graphic and illustrated. Coal #151A1D, cold slate #4A5961, institutional spruce-grey #566158, grey-paper #D2D2C5; tiny tungsten #A48B5D and oxide red #783F3B. Preserve readable black character silhouettes against middle-value surroundings. No photorealism, glossy 3D, cute chibi, anime, colorful neon, realistic detailed faces, historical political insignia or leaders, horror monsters, gore, magical effects, weapons focus, glowing outlined characters, decorative HUD clutter, debug labels, node graphs or software UI. All Chinese labels must be clean and legible; only use the short text specified for this screen, supporting print can be orderly thin rules. No watermark.
REQUESTED IMAGE: a single plausible exploration GAMEPLAY SCREEN of the hotel ground-floor lobby under a FIXED ORTHOGRAPHIC OBLIQUE OVERHEAD CAMERA, around 45 degrees azimuth and 40 degrees elevation. This MUST visibly differ from the old straight-on elevation: clearly show floor area, furniture tops, and TWO perpendicular interior walls. Intimate level-design composition filling the screen, not a floating miniature island or a flat side cutaway. Hide the near-facing walls, naturally crop building at screen edges. No rotating camera or cinematic lens distortion.
LAYOUT: winter double entrance doors with a vestibule and coat hooks at lower-left; a main reception counter diagonally across the upper-middle with guest side accessible; key rack, registry, telephone and weak lamp behind it. A waiting bench, locked ration cupboard and one worn rug create believable circulation; upper-left stairs rise to guest floors, a door behind reception leads to the office, a corridor recedes towards guest rooms on the right. Clear generous walkable paths. One warm desk light and cold window light shape the room. Propaganda leader poster above reception, state banner on wall, wall telescreen facing waiting area, reporting slot near a sealed notice board.
The clerk stands beside the counter in the foreground-middle as the controllable actor, Guest A waits at the counter, Guest B stands near the bench; all precisely preserve the symbolic figures. A small unobtrusive white interaction marker by the desk says "前台". Every character grounded on floor with restrained contact shadow.
MINIMAL GAME UI only: top-left small title "check in，please" and beneath "旅店大厅"; top-right small clock panel "第03天  08:40"; lower-left slim command strip "移动  ·  交谈  ·  查看". No big tutorial panels. Primary value is a compelling spacious hotel layout and inhabited authoritarian atmosphere.
